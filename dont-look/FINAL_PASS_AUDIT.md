# Final pass audit

The plot stays: Ben texts Chris a trail photo of Evan, Chris looks, the house is wrong, Mom is in the car, the voice is not her, and the car needs a fuse, the keys, gas, and a photo of the car. The writing, the opening, the doors, and the light on the floor do not match that story yet.

## Intro

The opening is three hard cuts: a phone, a photo swap, a paragraph. There is no drive, no breath before the text, and no fade into the mudroom. Skip works. The second bubble does not say who is typing.

## Text

- The first objective often says the basement door is next, because a missing key is treated as "not in the world." You have not found that key yet.
- Looking at the camera says "Ben, on the trail." The man in the hat is Evan. Ben sent the picture.
- Mom's note, the diary, the journal, and the scratched bench note are accurate but cramped. A few lines sound like notes to the programmer ("Seat the fuse").
- Captions use one duration for every line, so long thoughts vanish and short ones sit.
- "The slide won't stick in this browser" is a real failure and should stay plain.

## Doors

Seventeen doors, each one tile in a wall. They draw as a slab on the tile with no jamb, no swing, and no rattle when the lock holds. Collision is the tile: closed blocks, open does not. That part is right. The yard door is the wide motor door and should not look like a bedroom door. The basement door should not look like the hall doors.

## Shadows

Chris and the sleepers have no contact shadow. Nothing stretches away from the flashlight or the bench lamp. Props sit on the floor without a mark under them.

## Items

Each item has its own icon, but several still read as a small block at a distance, and nothing on the floor casts a shadow. Names match the notes. Descriptions are short to the point of being vague.

## Scares and sound

The grin reveal, the television, the mirror, and the fuse each flash and sting on the same frame. There is no room-cross fade. Footsteps already follow the step timer. Door audio fires when the flag flips, not with a swing. Locked doors only print a line.

## Loop

New game starts in the mudroom. Light is on the foyer table. Her key is in the bathroom cabinet. Car keys are in her room. The basement key is in Mom's sweater. The fuse, camera, revolver, and shed key are on the basement bench. Gas is in the shed, past the yard door, which needs the fuse. The photo is of the car, with Ben's camera, and then you hold to leave. Saves are film slides at the garage bench. Death returns to a slide if one exists. No credit roll; the ending card is the end.
