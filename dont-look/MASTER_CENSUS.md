# Master asset census

Audit of the build before this pass: floors were one repeating bake per material, furniture was a flat rectangle per kind, every shambler shared one body, items on the floor were 8px squares recolored by type, doors were a plank and a pixel, motes were single pixels, and most sounds were one noise burst plus one oscillator. The notes, lines, and ending were already right. They stay locked.

Every row below gets its own silhouette, material, or voice in this pass. Nothing in the story column changes.
